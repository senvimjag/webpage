About Me

Dr. J. Senthil Kumar is currently serving as Associate Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Mepco Schlenk Engineering College, Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, India.

He received his B.E. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Madurai Kamaraj University in 2003, M.E. in Communication Systems from Anna University in 2005, and Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Anna University in 2017.

He has more than 20 years of teaching and research experience with research contributions in Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Robotics, Deep Learning, Embedded Systems, Wireless Networks, Smart Healthcare, Metaverse, and Smart Cities.
