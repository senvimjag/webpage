About Me

Myself \textbf{Dr.J.Senthil Kumar, (Dr.Senthil Kumar Jagatheesaperumal) } have received my B.E. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Madurai Kamaraj University, Tamilnadu, India in 2003. Followed by that, I received my Post Graduation degree in Communication Systems from Anna University, Chennai, in 2005. Subsequently, I have received my Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Anna University, Chennai in 2017. With around 20 years of teaching experience and I am currently working as an Associate Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mepco Schlenk Engineering College, Sivakasi, Tamilnadu, India. I have received two funded research projects from National Instruments, USA each worth USD 50,000 during the years 2015 and 2016. Followed by that, I was also engaged in another funded research project from IITM-RUTAG during 2017 worth Rs.3.97 Lakhs. In 2024, I have received a sponsored research project from IHub-Data worth Rs. 8.2 lakhs. My core area of research includes Robotics, Internet of Things, Embedded Systems and Wireless Communication. I also hold five granted utility patents to my credit, and five more patents are under examination. During my 20 years of teaching and research, I have as published papers around 100 papers in reputed International Journals including IEEE, Elseiver and Springer publishers. Some of my key research publications have been published in IEEE Communication Surveys and Tutorials (IF 46.7), ACM Computing Surveys (IF 28.0), IEEE Wireless Communications (IF 11.5), IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IF 8.9), and IEEE Network (IF 6.3), etc. I am also a Member of IEEE, Life Member of IETE and ISTE.
