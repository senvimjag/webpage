About Me

Dr. J. Senthil Kumar received his B.E. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Madurai Kamaraj University in 2003, M.E. in Communication Systems from Anna University in 2005, and Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Anna University in 2017.

He is currently serving as Associate Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Mepco Schlenk Engineering College, Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, India.

He has published around 100 research papers in reputed international journals including IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, ACM, and Wiley. His research contributions span AI, IoT, Robotics, Deep Learning, Wireless Networks, Healthcare Systems, and Smart Cities.
